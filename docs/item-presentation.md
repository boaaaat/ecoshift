# Equipped item presentation

Current campaign gear uses `Shared.Art.OverhaulGearModels`. Handles, shafts, bindings and collars are round, with blade fullers, peened rivets, sockets and material variation. Each bow has 24 tapered limb sections and laminated inlays. These are native Roblox models; their editable sources are the Luau factories, not Blender exports.

`ItemAnimations.client.lua` composes arm poses after the character Animator. `ItemPose` drives joint `Transform` for R6 Motor6D rigs and current R15 AnimationConstraint rigs without modifying rig attachments. Locomotion continues on the rest of the body. Equip, unequip, death, distance culling and respawn restore the joints. Reduced Motion removes the optional release recoil; held bow ornaments also stop orbiting.

The pose families cover all current weapons and harvesting tools, plus food, drink, medicine, buckets and generic carried inventory items. Swords and sickles sweep, harvesting tools chop, spears thrust, hammers use a supporting hand, staves cast, and consumables rise toward the face. Locally accepted input predicts the motion immediately; server-accepted combat and harvest actions carry timestamps for other clients. These visual poses do not change damage, cooldowns or harvest rewards.

Bows use the right hand as the lead hand, as requested. The left hand draws the nocked arrow and string. The lead arm extends first; during the first .2 seconds the other hand approaches an untouched string. Once the hand reaches it, the string follows that hand and the limbs flex under tension. R6 follows its rigid-arm arc; R15 extends using actual arm lengths. Releasing hides the arrow and returns the limbs/string. The authoritative projectile origin uses the right side. Charge timing remains 1.3 seconds.

For an authored Tool prefab, place an `ItemGrip` Attachment in `Handle` at the grasp point. Its orientation defines the item's intended local orientation in the palm. Without it, non-generated prefabs retain their existing Tool grip offset and rotation. Bow rig coordinates use +X for arrow flight and +Y for the limb span.

After user feedback, the holding stance was rebuilt around a bent elbow and forward forearm. R15 ready positions derive from the neutral elbow and actual forearm/palm lengths; R6 rotates its rigid arm forward. The palm keeps a fixed relationship with the tool grip during actions. Picks, axes and sickles cut in a forward plane outside the torso. Picks now use a saved continuous curved CSG head, sickles a tapered hook, and swords/daggers closed pointed tips; their editable authoring sources are under `assets/models/tools/`.

The latest authorized suite passed **733 held inventory items, 44 gear factories, 8,868 pose samples and 189,545 checks**. It covers R6/R15 grasp and wrist contact, forward ready forearms, stable grip rotation, mining head clearance, support hands, bow approach/draw/release/cancel, and joint restoration. Numerical contact checks alone did not catch the earlier unattractive hanging-arm stance; the new posture regressions and live visual review address that gap.

Live review covered representatives of all 15 pose families on the player's R15 avatar. A fresh Play session confirmed the saved normal-inventory Harvester model/pose, mouse-triggered harvest motion, right-handed bow draw/release and unequip cancellation. The bow's empty left arm relaxes at idle; the holding arm remains bent forward. Screenshots: [Harvester ready](images/pickaxe-ready.png), [right-handed bow draw](images/bow-right-draw.png). Some generic carried resources and consumables still use simple placeholder geometry; this correction pass validates their poses, not finished unique artwork for every inventory item. See [validation results](art-validation.md) for coverage and limits.

The R15 joint implementation follows the current [Roblox AnimationConstraint documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/AnimationConstraint), including procedural Transform updates after Animator evaluation.
