## Confirmed archive policy

Each original expedition participant has one personally named copy of the same shared world, up to five copies per player. Every original member needs a free slot to create a world, which records the actual 1–6 players at Start. Matchmaking now merges a lobby crew without creating a world or consuming a save slot. Removing a copy affects only that player's archive; resuming with the original crew recreates missing copies when those players have capacity. Any original owner with a remaining copy may request resume once every original player is together, online and ready. Party leadership may change.
